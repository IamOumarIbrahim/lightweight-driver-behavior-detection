DATA PLACEMENT
Put the licensed DMD folder in data/DMD and Drive&Act in data/Drive&Act. These media folders and data/processed are local-only and ignored by Git.

PUBLISHED DATA
data/annotations contains portable authored annotations and frozen split metadata. Run scripts/data in numeric order to unpack, derive frames, and verify integrity.

LICENSES
DMD and Drive&Act remain subject to their original licenses and access terms. This repository publishes annotations and split metadata, not source frames or videos.
